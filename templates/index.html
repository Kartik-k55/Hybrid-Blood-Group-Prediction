Authentic 5-Stage System Architecture Breakdown

Comprehensive technical and mathematical explanation of each processing phase implemented in the system.

Ingests biometric impressions from optical, capacitive, or synthetic sources in standard formats (BMP, PNG, JPG). Optional authenticated AES-256-GCM encrypted payload decryption guarantees tamper-evident transport integrity before image buffer formulation.

Converts impressions to single-channel 8-bit luminance. Applies Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE, clip limit 2.5, 8×8 grid) to sharpen epidermal ridge contours without amplifying sensor grain noise. Resizes image via bicubic interpolation to 224 × 224 × 3 and subtracts ImageNet channel means.

Feeds preprocessed tensors through fine-tuned VGG16 (Blocks 1-4 frozen, Block 5 fine-tuned at lr=1e-5). Global Average Pooling (GAP) extracts 512-dimensional continuous latent feature representations. In parallel, computes gradient orientation coherence, ridge density, and Henry pattern prior distribution (Loop, Whorl, Arch, Composite).

Concatenates the deep 512-D vector with ridge biometrics, applies standard scaling, and reduces dimensionality via PCA retaining 95% cumulative variance. Radial Basis Function (RBF) Support Vector Machine predicts raw decision scores, which are adjusted via Bayesian population calibration against real-world ABO/Rh prevalence ratios.

Presents the final predicted ABO and Rh blood group accompanied by Bayesian posterior confidence percentages, Shannon entropy uncertainty index, and universal donor/recipient matching guidelines with mandatory non-clinical research disclaimers.
